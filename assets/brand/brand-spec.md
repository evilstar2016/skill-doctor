# Skill Doctor brand mark

## Purpose

The mark combines a diagnostic pulse, a continuous scan, and a subtle `S`. It expresses Skill Doctor as a precise, calm instrument for finding and repairing problems in AI-agent skills.

## Files

- `skill-doctor-mark.svg` — standalone icon for app icons, avatars, and compact placements.
- `skill-doctor-logo.svg` — horizontal lockup for headers, documents, and social profiles.

## Colour

| Use | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Clinical emerald | `#15925F` |
| Forest ink | `#16201A` |
| Light background | `#F3F6F4` |

Use the supplied SVGs on white or very light neutral backgrounds. The mark is transparent; do not add shadows, gradients, or decorative containers.

## Spacing and size

- Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the mark height on every side.
- Use the standalone mark no smaller than 20 px wide.
- Use the horizontal lockup no smaller than 150 px wide.

## Do not

- Recolour the mark outside the approved emerald and forest-ink system.
- Stretch, rotate, crop, or separate the pulse from the scan.
- Use the wordmark without its supplied visual proportions.

The visual language follows the existing clinical command-centre palette in `web/DESIGN.md`.
